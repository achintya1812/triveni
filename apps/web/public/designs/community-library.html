A community audio library, on Telegram

Today the Bhili is machine-made. Local speakers turn it into real voices, and fix the words.

  • One line at a time. The recorder bot sends a speaker a script line; they reply with a voice note.
  • A second speaker checks it with one tap: right, or record again.
  • Approved recordings replace the generated audio for every class that uses the line, and corrections to the Bhili text go back into the content.
  • Who records: the teacher, the anganwadi worker, a parent, an older student.
  • Next languages: Gondi and Kurukh need their own speech models; the library can start with people's voices.

The script line is the app's own Bhili (from the borrowing kit). The names and counts are illustrative.
