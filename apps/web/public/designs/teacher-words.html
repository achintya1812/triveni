The teacher meets the children midway

The child moves toward Hindi. Triveni also moves the teacher toward Bhili, a few words at a time.

  • Tomorrow's words, the evening before. A five-minute drill of the next lesson's key words, from the same audio the children hear. Classroom phrases ("sit down", "well done") come first.
  • Spaced reviews. Each word comes back after 1 day, 3 days and a week, just before she'd forget it.
  • Support that fades. As she learns words, the reteaching kit hands those lines from the audio to her.
  • Pronunciation checked by people. She compares her attempt with the local speaker's recording.

About 10 words a topic × 2 topics a week × 30 weeks ≈ 600 words met in a year. Not fluency, but enough for instructions, praise and core ideas. Her progress is private and optional. The Bhili words are the app's own (machine-translated, awaiting a Bhili speaker); the counts are illustrative.
